10 Principles of Good Design
01
Good design is innovative
It does not exhaust itself in its own originality, but is always in an open, forward-looking relationship with technology.
Claimed
02
Good design makes a product useful
A product is bought to be used. It has to satisfy not only functional, but also psychological and aesthetic criteria.
Claimed
03
Good design is aesthetic
The aesthetic quality of a product is integral to its usefulness, because products we use every day affect our wellbeing.
Claimed
04
Good design makes a product understandable
It clarifies the product's structure. Better still, it can make the product clearly express its function by making use of the user's intuition.
Claimed
05
Good design is unobtrusive
Products fulfilling a purpose are like tools. They are neither decorative objects nor works of art.
Claimed
06
Good design is honest
It does not make a product more innovative, powerful or valuable than it really is. It does not attempt to manipulate the consumer.
Claimed
07
Good design is long-lasting
It avoids being fashionable and therefore never appears antiquated. Unlike fashionable design, it lasts many years.
Claimed
08
Good design is thorough down to the last detail
Nothing must be arbitrary or left to chance. Care and accuracy in the design process show respect towards the consumer.
Claimed
09
Good design is environmentally-friendly
Design makes an important contribution to the preservation of the environment. It conserves resources and minimises physical and visual pollution.
Claimed
10
Good design is as little design as possible
Less, but better — because it concentrates on the essential aspects, and the products are not burdened with non-essentials.
Claimed
