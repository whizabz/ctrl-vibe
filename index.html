vibe cod·ing
/vīb ˈkōdiNG/ verb, gerund
1.

Building software by describing what you want in natural language and iterating on what comes back. You are not writing code line by line — you are directing. The skill is not syntax; it is knowing what to ask for, how to read what you get back, and when to push further.

"Think of it like working with a contractor who can build anything but needs very clear briefs, hates ambiguity, and forgets everything you told them last week."
